Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you send your first request.

Is A2Z KAR available outside Meerut?

Abhi service area Meerut hai. Other locations ke requests currently accept nahi kiye ja rahe.

How much will I have to pay?

Starting service fee ₹99 hai for the launch offer. Any third-party purchase, repair or travel expense is separate where applicable. We will discuss the expected amount and take approval before proceeding.

Can I request help for my parents or family?

Yes. Aap kisi family member ke liye request raise kar sakte hain. Please share only the details needed to coordinate the task.

Will you guarantee that every task gets completed?

We first check feasibility, timing and local availability. We will confirm whether we can take up the task; not every request can be guaranteed.

Can I pay online?

Payment method and amount will be shared after your request is reviewed and confirmed. Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone.

Is A2Z KAR an emergency medical service?

No. A2Z KAR does not provide medical advice, ambulance service or emergency response. For urgent medical or safety emergencies, contact the appropriate emergency service directly.
